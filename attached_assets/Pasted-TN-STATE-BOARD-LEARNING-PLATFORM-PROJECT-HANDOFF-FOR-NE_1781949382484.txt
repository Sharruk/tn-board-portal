====================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW
================

Project Name:
TN State Board Learning Platform

Purpose:

This is NOT a generic LMS.

This platform is designed for Tamil Nadu State Board students.

The primary workflow is:

Teacher conducts exam
↓
Question Paper is received
↓
Question Paper PDF uploaded
↓
YouTube explanation video uploaded
↓
Students watch video
↓
Students download PDF
↓
Students download answer key

The website acts as the central hub between YouTube and downloadable educational resources.

====================================================
BUSINESS GOAL
